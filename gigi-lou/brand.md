# GIGI LOU — Brand Aesthetic

## Mood
Luxury home decor & lifestyle. Rich, luxe, quiet luxury. Calm, confident, never loud.

## Content ideas
- Styled corners: shelves, coffee tables, consoles
- Before / after room refreshes
- Close-up texture shots: linen, marble, travertine, brass
- "How to style" reels: 3 pieces, 1 surface
- Candle-lit evening scenes, morning light scenes

## Logo
Stacked geometric monogram **Gi / G** with a hand-written script **Lou** underneath.
Files: `assets/logo/` — stone mark on oxblood.
- Use the stone mark on oxblood (primary) or the oxblood mark on ivory/stone.
- Keep clear space around it equal to the dot of the "i".
- Never stretch, recolour, or place on busy photos.

## Type
- Headlines: bold geometric sans in short lines (Montserrat Bold)
- Accent word: flowing script, one or two words only ("Well", "the vision")
- Body: Montserrat Regular / Light

## Campaign style
- Headline formula: **bold sans line** + *script word* — "Place it *Well*", "Ordinary was never *the vision*"
- Sculptural product as hero, styled with luxe props (pearls, crystal glassware, antique books, brass sconces)
- Backdrops: sand/beige paper with fine white line waves, or deep oxblood interiors
- Small stacked logo placed in the frame, never large

## Voice
- Short sentences. Few words. Let it breathe.
- Words to use: curated, timeless, refined, signature, effortless, elevated
- Avoid: "cheap", "sale!!!", heavy emoji, all-caps shouting
